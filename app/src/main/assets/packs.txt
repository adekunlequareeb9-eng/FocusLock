## Arabic grammar basics
Q: What are the three kinds of words (الكلمة)?
A: اسم، فعل، حرف
Q: What are the three cases (الإعراب) of a noun?
A: رفع، نصب، جر
Q: What is the sign of الرفع on a regular singular noun?
A: الضمة
Q: What is the sign of النصب on a regular singular noun?
A: الفتحة
Q: What is the sign of الجر on a regular singular noun?
A: الكسرة
Q: What case is the مبتدأ (subject of a nominal sentence) in?
A: مرفوع
Q: What case is the خبر (predicate) of a simple nominal sentence in?
A: مرفوع
Q: What case is the فاعل (doer of the verb) in?
A: مرفوع
Q: What case is the مفعول به (direct object) in?
A: منصوب
Q: What case is a noun right after a preposition (حرف جر) in?
A: مجرور
Q: What does a nominal sentence (الجملة الاسمية) begin with?
A: اسم
Q: What does a verbal sentence (الجملة الفعلية) begin with?
A: فعل
Q: What is the effect of إنّ on the sentence?
A: تنصب الاسم وترفع الخبر
Q: What is the effect of كان on the sentence?
A: ترفع الاسم وتنصب الخبر

## Coding basics
Q: In Python, which keyword defines a function?
A: def
Q: What does len([1, 2, 3]) return in Python?
A: 3
Q: In Kotlin, which keyword declares a read-only variable?
A: val
Q: In Kotlin, which keyword declares a variable that can change?
A: var
Q: What does HTML stand for?
A: HyperText Markup Language
Q: What does CSS stand for?
A: Cascading Style Sheets
Q: What does API stand for?
A: Application Programming Interface
Q: What does git commit do?
A: Saves your staged changes as a snapshot in history
Q: What does === check in JavaScript?
A: Same value and same type
Q: What is the index of the first item in a Python list?
A: 0
